Gnocchi with Spring Vegetables

Description

This delicious gnocchi recipe is both healthy and delicious.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 8 oz mushrooms
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1/2 bundle asparagus
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 4 tbsp salted butter
  • 16 oz potato gnocchi
  • 2/3 cup frozen peas
  • 1/2 lemon
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Gather and prepare all ingredients.
  2. Heat 1 Tbsp of oil in a large cast-iron over medium heat. Once hot, add the quartered mushrooms. Cook them undisturbed for 3 minutes.
  3. Next, add the remaining oil, onions, asparagus, garlic, salt, and pepper. Cook for 3 minutes until the onions start to soften.
  4. Push the vegetables to one side of the cast iron. Melt 2 Tbsp of butter in the open space. Add the gnocchi in a single layer to the butter and cook undisturbed for about 3 minutes.
  5. Then, add the remaining butter, flip the gnocchi, and continue cooking for another 3 minutes until golden brown. I like to use a rubber spatula to gently flip the gnocchi.
  6. Add the peas and squeeze in the fresh lemon juice, and cook for about 2 minutes to warm through.
  7. Sprinkle on the Parmesan cheese and enjoy!
